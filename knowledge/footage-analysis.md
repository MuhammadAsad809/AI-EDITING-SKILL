# Footage Analysis

Tag each clip before editing. Record shot size, subject, camera movement, subject movement, direction, lighting, focus, composition, emotional intensity, technical quality, and likely editorial role.

## Placement heuristics

- Wide shots establish geography, scale, or a reset.
- Medium shots carry readable action and interaction.
- Close-ups carry emotion, detail, or impact.
- Moving shots bridge energy; static shots can create attention or release.
- A shot with clean screen direction can connect action; a reverse direction may be reserved for a deliberate break or peak.
- A technically imperfect but emotionally decisive shot may be a hero; protect it with a short duration, crop, or motivated texture rather than discarding it automatically.

Score candidates for clarity, emotional value, motion, uniqueness, technical quality, and continuity. Select the highest editorial value, not simply the highest resolution.
