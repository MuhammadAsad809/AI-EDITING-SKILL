# Text and Typography

Text has a job: identify, clarify, emphasize, or entertain. Establish hierarchy between title, subtitle, caption, and metadata. Use a legible typeface, sufficient contrast, safe margins, and enough display time for the reading speed.

For captions, break lines at natural phrases, avoid covering faces or key action, and emphasize only meaningful words. Word-by-word animation is useful for speech-led short-form content but becomes visual noise when every syllable is animated. Beat-synced text should enter on a phrase or accent, not merely because a beat occurred.

Check vertical, square, and horizontal crops independently. Keep critical text inside platform-safe areas and provide captions when dialogue carries meaning.
