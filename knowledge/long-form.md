# Long-Form Editing

For interviews, tutorials, podcasts, documentaries, and YouTube essays, build a durable structure before polishing. Create a transcript or paper edit, mark the thesis and chapter turns, remove repetition, then add B-roll and graphics where they clarify the idea.

Use J-cuts to preview the next chapter, L-cuts to preserve emotional continuity, and room tone to smooth removals. Retention comes from progression: a new question, example, visual, consequence, or emotional turn. Avoid false urgency created by constant punch-ins and sound hits.

Maintain a style bible for captions, lower thirds, music levels, color, and recurring transitions. Check the first minute, chapter openings, and final minute separately before watching the entire export.
