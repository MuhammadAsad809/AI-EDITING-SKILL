# Short-Form Editing

Design for the first second without sacrificing a coherent promise. Open with a result, unusual image, question, or emotional state. Use pattern interrupts when attention is likely to drop: a framing change, visual proof, caption shift, sound change, or cutaway.

Keep captions readable, prioritize the spoken idea, and make the payoff arrive before the viewer has been promised too much. A loop ending should connect naturally to the opening, not feel like a missing conclusion. Place a CTA after value or at a credible transition.

Evaluate retention by moments of repeated information, dead air, unclear context, and unmotivated visual noise. “Fast” is useful only when it increases information or energy.
