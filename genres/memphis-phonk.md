# Classic Memphis Phonk

Classic Memphis Phonk benefits from hypnotic repetition, lo-fi atmosphere, and contrast rather than constant speed. Use longer shots, texture, silhouette, and natural or sampled ambience. A hard cut can still be powerful when it breaks a held rhythm.

Use warm/desaturated grading, grain, restrained VHS artifacts, and slow push-ins only when they support the period or mood. Let the track’s vocal sample or bass phrase motivate the strongest visual change. Avoid turning every section into drift-style hypercutting.
