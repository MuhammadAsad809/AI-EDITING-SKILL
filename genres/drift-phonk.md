# Drift Phonk

Drift Phonk centers on mechanical power, lateral movement, dark atmosphere, and bass impact. Establish the car before accelerating the edit. Use silhouette, wheel, cockpit, road, and hero-pass coverage to create scale and continuity.

## Editorial recipe

- Hook with a taillight, wheel, or decisive motion—not an unreadable flash.
- Build with 2–4 beat holds and matched movement direction.
- Place the hero pass on the drop; use one ramp, impact, or short shake.
- Release with a stable slow-motion detail or held hero frame.
- Grade for readable blacks, protected highlights, and selective neon accents.

Avoid cutting every cowbell, hiding the car under overlays, and using spin/zoom presets when the camera direction does not match.
