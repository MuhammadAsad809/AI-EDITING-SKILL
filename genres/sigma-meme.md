# Sigma / Meme Phonk

Sigma and meme edits rely on character recognition, attitude, timing, and graphic punctuation. Establish the face or action, use a pause or reaction to create expectation, then land the caption, crop, freeze frame, or bass accent.

Use text for the joke or idea, not as a permanent wallpaper. Keep the face legible, captions inside safe areas, and freeze frames short enough to preserve momentum. Avoid skulls, glitch, and chromatic aberration on every beat; one clear motif is more memorable than a preset stack.
