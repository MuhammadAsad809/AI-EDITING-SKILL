# Travel Editing

Move from place identity to sensory detail to human experience. Use a location reveal, directional movement, ambient sound, and a memorable closing image. Sync broad changes to musical phrases, but let natural sound briefly lead when it adds presence.

## Planning checklist

- Define the viewer promise and hero moment.
- Tag clips by shot size, movement, emotional value, and technical limits.
- Build a section-level arc before adding beat subdivisions.
- Identify two or three signature visual or sound motifs.
- Reserve the strongest effect, sound, and grade contrast for the peak.
- Review muted, audio-only, and at platform size.

## Example decision

If a clip is visually exciting but obscures the subject, use it as a short texture or bridge and place a clearer shot on the structural beat. If a shot carries the story but is technically weak, shorten it, stabilize or crop conservatively, and support it with sound rather than replacing its meaning with effects.
