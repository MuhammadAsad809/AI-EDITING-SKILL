# Gym / Hype Phonk

Gym and hype edits should make effort readable: preparation, exertion, decisive action, reaction, and result. Use wide or medium framing to establish the movement, then close-ups for grip, breath, strain, or impact.

Keep natural breath, plates, footsteps, or room tone under the music when they reinforce effort. Use a speed ramp only around a meaningful lift, sprint, punch, or pose. Reserve zoom pulses and shake for the strongest landing; do not obscure technique or safety-critical movement.
