# Example: 25-Second Anime AMV

Choose the character’s emotional turn as the narrative spine. Use a close-up on the lyric that names the feeling, a matched movement into action, and an impact frame on the chorus downbeat. Avoid stacking flash, shake, chromatic aberration, and glow; choose one accent based on the shot’s existing motion.

Use a warm-to-cool grade shift only if it marks a story change. Let one breath or held reaction after the peak prevent the sequence from becoming an undifferentiated wall of action.
