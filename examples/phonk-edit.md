# Example: 20-Second Phonk Car Edit

## PROJECT ANALYSIS
Music-led vertical edit. Assumption: exterior passes, wheel details, interior, and a hero driving shot.

## EDITING STYLE
Dark, bass-led, controlled aggression. Visual motif: circular wheel movement and red taillight accents.

## TIMELINE
00:00–00:02: cold-open taillight detail with engine texture; hold long enough to identify the car.

00:02–00:06: build with exterior silhouette, wheel, interior, and mirror shots on phrase accents.

00:06: drop: hard cut to hero pass, 150% acceleration into the movement, then return to normal on the bass landing.

00:06–00:15: alternate movement direction and detail scale; use one 2–4 frame shake on a bass hit, not on every kick.

00:15–00:18: brief slow motion on wheel or body roll; let engine sound lead the next cut.

00:18–00:20: final hero angle with restrained title or model name; grade consistency and reflections take priority over a heavy LUT.
