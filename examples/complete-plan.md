# Complete Plan Fixture: 20-Second Phonk Car Montage

## PROJECT ANALYSIS
- Promise: reveal a performance car as a controlled, nocturnal machine.
- Platform: vertical social video; 20 seconds; 9:16; 30 fps.
- Known footage: exterior passes, wheel details, interior, and one hero drive.
- Assumptions: music is 150 BPM; verify the grid before cutting.

## EDITING STYLE
Music-led, dark, bass-driven, with a measured build and one clear peak. Story and vehicle readability come before effects.

## MUSIC / BPM
At 150 BPM, one beat is 400 ms. Use downbeats for section changes, kicks for movement, and bass hits for the hero landing. Do not cut every hi-hat.

## TIMELINE
| Timecode | Section | Shot / clip | Cut reason | Audio cue | Effect strength |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 00:00–00:02 | Hook | Taillight close-up | Establish identity before speed | Engine texture | None |
| 00:02–00:06 | Setup | Silhouette, wheel, interior | Build visual vocabulary | Low-pass music build | Subtle 3% push-in |
| 00:06–00:10 | Escalation | Exterior pass and mirror detail | Increase movement and scale contrast | Kicks and tire texture | One 150% ramp |
| 00:10 | Peak | Hero driving pass | Land the drop on the clearest action | Bass impact | One short shake |
| 00:10–00:17 | Release | Wheel/body-roll slow motion | Let the result read | Engine continues | 50% slow motion |
| 00:17–00:20 | Ending | Final hero angle and model title | Close or loop the promise | Music resolves | No transition |

## CUT PLAN
Use hard cuts except where directionally matched movement supports continuity. Cut the hero pass on the bass landing, not before the car is legible.

## TRANSITION PLAN
No decorative transitions. Use a cut for momentum; the only speed transition is motivated by the moving car entering the drop.

## SPEED PLAN
Hold natural speed through setup, accelerate into the hero pass, land at normal speed, then slow to 50% for the release. Add motion blur only if interpolation produces clean edges.

## MOTION PLAN
Use a 3% push-in on the taillight and a restrained 2-frame directional shake at the peak. Do not shake the release or text.

## SOUND DESIGN
Keep engine and tire texture audible under the build. Add one designed bass impact on the hero landing, duck music briefly under the engine transient, and use ambience to connect locations.

## COLOR GRADING
Balance mixed cameras first, then use cool shadows, controlled highlights, and selective red taillight saturation. Protect reflections and skin tones if a driver is visible.

## TEXT / TYPOGRAPHY
Place the model name inside the vertical safe area for 1.5 seconds during the final hero hold. Use one bold sans-serif style with high contrast and no word-by-word animation.

## SOFTWARE-SPECIFIC STEPS
- [PREMIERE PRO] Mark the 150 BPM grid, use a nested hero shot for the ramp, and use Lumetri for shot matching.
- [CAPCUT] Use speed curves and an adjustment layer; verify the title remains inside the mobile safe area.

## EXPORT SETTINGS
Deliver a 9:16 H.264 review file at 1080×1920, source frame rate, platform-appropriate bitrate, and a high-quality master if requested. Verify current platform limits before publishing.

## FINAL QUALITY CHECK
Confirm that every effect has a purpose, the hero is the strongest moment, the car remains readable, the audio is not clipped, text is legible on a phone, color is consistent, and the export matches the target aspect ratio.
