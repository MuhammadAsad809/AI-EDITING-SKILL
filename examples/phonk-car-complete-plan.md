# Complete Example: 20-Second Drift Phonk Car Edit

## PROJECT ANALYSIS
Vertical short-form edit for a black performance coupe. Promise: make the car feel powerful and controlled, not merely fast. Known footage: taillight detail, wheel close-up, interior, side pass, and one hero drift. Assumption: 150 BPM; verify the track grid.

## EDITING STYLE
Drift Phonk with a dark, high-contrast look. Use a calm two-beat hook, a phrase-led build, one decisive drop, and a short release. Motifs are circular wheel movement and red taillight accents.

## MUSIC / BPM
At 150 BPM, a quarter beat is 400 ms and an eighth is 200 ms. Use bars for section changes, bass hits for weight, and selected cowbells for detail changes. Do not cut every hi-hat.

## TIMELINE

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## CUT PLAN
Prefer hard cuts. Cut during visible movement when the next shot completes the action. Keep screen direction consistent until the drop, where a controlled direction break can signal impact.

## TRANSITION PLAN
No decorative transitions. The only transition is a velocity bridge into the hero pass because the movement is continuous; use a clean cut if the match fails.

## SPEED PLAN
Natural speed through setup; accelerate 130–180% into the hero; land at normal speed on the bass hit; slow to 50% for release. Use eased time-remapping points and inspect wheel geometry before enabling interpolation.

## MOTION PLAN
Use a 3% push-in on the hook and one 2-frame directional shake at the peak. Add motion blur only to the ramp. Keep the ending stable so the title is readable.

## SOUND DESIGN
Preserve engine and tire texture. Add one bass impact on the hero landing, a short whoosh for the visible pass, and low-level ambience to connect camera positions. Duck music only briefly if engine detail is the emotional point.

## COLOR GRADING
Match cameras first. Use cool shadows, controlled highlights, deep but recoverable blacks, and selective red taillight saturation. Do not crush reflections or apply a LUT without checking the source color space.

## TEXT / TYPOGRAPHY
Place the model name inside the 9:16 safe area for 1.5 seconds during the final hold. Use one bold sans-serif style; no word-by-word animation.

## SOFTWARE-SPECIFIC STEPS
- [CAPCUT] Mark the beat grid, use a custom speed curve, and apply the grade on an adjustment layer.
- [ALIGHT MOTION] Precompose the hero ramp, keyframe scale/position, and keep the shake layer separate.
- [PREMIERE PRO] Use markers, a nested hero shot, Time Remapping, Lumetri, and Essential Sound.
- [DAVINCI RESOLVE] Use Fairlight for engine/music automation and Color nodes for shot matching.

## AI-ASSET PLAN
No generated hero footage is needed. AI may be used to generate a subtle grain or title concept only if the real footage already carries the action. Verify any enhancement for wheel, reflection, and logo artifacts.

## EXPORT SETTINGS
Deliver 1080×1920 H.264 at the source frame-rate strategy, with a platform-appropriate bitrate. Keep a high-quality master and verify current platform limits before publishing.

## FINAL QUALITY CHECK
Confirm the car is recognizable before the drop, the hero is the strongest moment, the drop has contrast, every effect has a reason, no flash pattern is unsafe, the engine is not clipped, the title is readable on a phone, and the export matches 9:16.
