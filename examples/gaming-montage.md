# Example: 30-Second Gaming Montage

## PROJECT ANALYSIS
A music-led, aggressive highlight reel at 150 BPM. Assumption: 16:9 gameplay with isolated game audio and one strongest clutch.

## MUSIC / BPM
A beat is 400 ms; an eighth is 200 ms. Use bars for section changes and reserve sixteenth-level flashes for the drop peak.

## TIMELINE
| Time | Section | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 00:00–00:02 | Hook | Open on the clutch’s decisive frame, then brief pre-action rewind | Immediate promise and context |
| 00:02–00:05 | Setup | Three medium holds, one per half-bar | Establish player, location, stakes |
| 00:05–00:12 | Build | Cuts on selected kicks and snares; reaction insert after the second highlight | Increase information, not random speed |
| 00:12–00:15 | Peak | Ramp into the clutch, freeze on impact, bass hit | Reserve maximum contrast |
| 00:15–00:27 | Escalation/release | Shorter clips, then a two-beat hold on the final result | Let payoff read |
| 00:27–00:30 | Ending | Clean logo or loop to opening frame | Closure without dead air |

Use a hard cut by default, a 4–8 frame impact flash only at the peak, and game audio under the decisive action.
