# Phonk Car Montage Workflow

1. **Define the promise:** power, precision, menace, luxury, or drift energy.
2. **Audit footage:** group exterior silhouette, driving direction, wheel/detail, interior, engine, reflections, and hero passes.
3. **Analyze the track:** record BPM, bar length, intro, build, drop, bass hits, cowbells, vocal accents, and release.
4. **Choose the hero:** select the clearest, most emotionally valuable pass; reserve it for the drop.
5. **Build a silent arc:** taillight or detail hook → silhouette setup → movement build → detail escalation → hero drop → slow/reaction release → final identity shot.
6. **Place structural markers:** phrase and bar changes first; use downbeats for shot-size or scene changes.
7. **Add accents:** kicks move or cut when meaningful; 808s add weight; cowbells can trigger detail changes; avoid mechanical sixteenth cuts.
8. **Design velocity:** baseline through setup, accelerate into the pass, land at natural speed, then slow the strongest body roll or wheel detail.
9. **Sound design:** keep engine/tire texture, add one designed bass impact, use a whoosh only for visible movement, and duck music briefly if natural sound is the point.
10. **Grade and texture:** match cameras, protect reflections, choose one look, then add restrained grain/VHS/neon accents according to subgenre.
11. **Review:** muted for visual arc, audio-only for impact hierarchy, phone-size for subject and title legibility.
12. **Export:** render the target aspect ratio and source frame-rate strategy, then verify current platform limits.
