# Montage Workflow

1. Write the promise and choose a hero moment.
2. Analyze song sections, BPM, downbeats, accents, and energy.
3. Tag footage and build a selects stringout.
4. Create a silent seven-part arc.
5. Place structural edits on bars and phrases.
6. Add accented cuts on meaningful kicks, snares, bass, and vocals.
7. Design anticipation, peak, and release.
8. Add purposeful speed, motion, sound, color, and text.
9. Test clean-cut, muted, audio-only, and platform-size versions.
10. Export a review file, fix notes, then deliver a final with project archive.
